# Credit Glossary (Sample)

> **SYNTHETIC SAMPLE DOCUMENT.** This file is fictional demo data for the Lending Copilot
> portfolio project. Any resemblance to real institutions, products, or persons is coincidental.

- **CIBIL score:** a 3-digit credit score (300-900) issued by TransUnion CIBIL summarising
  a borrower's credit history. Higher is better; 750+ is considered excellent.
- **DPD (days past due):** the number of days a payment is overdue. 30 DPD means one missed EMI cycle.
- **DTI (debt-to-income ratio):** total monthly debt obligations divided by net monthly income,
  expressed as a percentage.
- **EMI (equated monthly instalment):** the fixed monthly payment covering principal and interest.
- **LTV (loan-to-value ratio):** the loan amount divided by the appraised value of the collateral,
  expressed as a percentage.
- **NPA (non-performing asset):** a loan account where interest or principal has remained overdue
  for more than 90 days.
- **Moratorium:** a temporary pause in EMI payments granted during borrower hardship.
- **Restructuring:** modification of loan terms (tenure, EMI, rate) for borrowers in genuine distress.
- **PEP (politically exposed person):** an individual entrusted with a prominent public function;
  such customers require enhanced due diligence under AML norms.
- **Structuring (smurfing):** splitting transactions to stay below reporting thresholds;
  a classic money-laundering red flag.
- **Write-off:** an accounting entry removing a loan from the active books after prolonged
  non-payment; it does not extinguish the borrower's liability.
- **FIU-IND:** the Financial Intelligence Unit of India, which receives cash and suspicious
  transaction reports from lenders.
