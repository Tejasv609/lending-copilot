# Interest Rate Card (Sample)

> **SYNTHETIC SAMPLE DOCUMENT.** This file is fictional demo data for the Lending Copilot
> portfolio project. Any resemblance to real institutions, products, or persons is coincidental.

## 1. Personal loans

| CIBIL band | Interest rate (reducing balance, p.a.) |
|------------|----------------------------------------|
| 750+ | 11.5% - 13.0% |
| 700 - 749 | 13.5% - 15.5% |
| 650 - 699 | 16.0% - 18.5% |

Minimum loan amount INR 50,000; maximum INR 25,00,000.

## 2. Home loans

- Salaried: 8.75% p.a. onwards (floating).
- Self-employed: 9.25% p.a. onwards (floating).
- Maximum loan-to-value (LTV): 80% for loans up to INR 30,00,000, 75% above that.

## 3. Credit cards

- Revolving credit: 3.49% per month (42% p.a.).
- Interest-free period: up to 45 days on retail purchases.

## 4. Fees and charges

- Processing fee: 2% of the loan amount plus GST, capped at INR 25,000.
- Prepayment charges: 4% of the outstanding principal if prepaid within 12 months of disbursement;
  nil thereafter for floating-rate loans.
- Late payment fee: INR 500 or 2% of the overdue EMI, whichever is higher.
- Cheque / ECS bounce charges: INR 450 per instance.

## 5. Rate revision

Floating rates are reset every 6 months linked to the benchmark rate. Fixed-rate personal
loans do not change during the tenure.
