# Retail Credit Policy (Sample)

> **SYNTHETIC SAMPLE DOCUMENT.** This file is fictional demo data for the Lending Copilot
> portfolio project. Any resemblance to real institutions, products, or persons is coincidental.

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets the underwriting standards for unsecured retail lending products:
personal loans, credit cards, and consumer durable loans.

## 2. Customer eligibility

- Applicant must be an Indian resident aged 21 to 60 at loan maturity.
- Minimum 12 months of continuous employment (salaried) or 24 months of business vintage (self-employed).
- Minimum net monthly income: INR 25,000 for personal loans, INR 20,000 for credit cards.
- The applicant must not have any loan account classified as NPA in the last 36 months.

## 3. Credit bureau assessment

Applications are assessed against the applicant's CIBIL score:

| CIBIL score | Decision |
|-------------|----------|
| 750 and above | Approve, best pricing tier |
| 700 - 749 | Approve, standard pricing |
| 650 - 699 | Refer to credit manager with additional income proof |
| Below 650 | Decline |

A minimum of 6 months of bureau history is required. Thin-file applicants
(fewer than 3 tradelines) are referred for manual review.

## 4. Debt-to-income (DTI) limits

- Total DTI including the proposed EMI must not exceed 50% of net monthly income.
- For applicants with CIBIL above 750, DTI up to 55% may be approved by a credit manager.

## 5. Loan amounts and tenure

- Personal loan amounts range from INR 50,000 to INR 25,00,000.
- Maximum tenure is 60 months for personal loans and 48 months for consumer durable loans.
- Top-up loans are permitted only after 12 EMIs have been paid on the base loan.

## 6. Approval authority matrix

- Up to INR 5,00,000: automated decision engine.
- INR 5,00,001 to INR 15,00,000: credit manager.
- Above INR 15,00,000: regional credit head.
