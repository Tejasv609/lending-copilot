# KYC and AML Checklist (Sample)

> **SYNTHETIC SAMPLE DOCUMENT.** This file is fictional demo data for the Lending Copilot
> portfolio project. Any resemblance to real institutions, products, or persons is coincidental.

## 1. Customer identification (KYC)

Every borrower must complete KYC before disbursement:

- **Identity proof:** PAN card is mandatory. Aadhaar, passport, or voter ID as secondary proof.
- **Address proof:** Aadhaar, utility bill not older than 2 months, or registered rent agreement.
- **Photograph:** one recent passport-size photograph.
- For loans above INR 10,00,000, physical verification of the residence or office address is required.

## 2. Beneficial ownership

For non-individual borrowers (proprietorships, partnerships, companies), identify all
beneficial owners holding 10% or more of capital or profits.

## 3. AML red flags

Escalate to the compliance team immediately if any of the following are observed:

- Customer is reluctant to provide KYC documents or provides inconsistent information.
- Transactions structured just below reporting thresholds (structuring / smurfing).
- Rapid movement of funds with no clear economic purpose.
- Customer appears on a sanctions or PEP (politically exposed person) list.

## 4. Transaction monitoring thresholds

- Cash transactions of INR 10,00,000 and above in a month must be reported.
- All suspicious transactions must be reported to the FIU-IND within the statutory timeline,
  regardless of amount.

## 5. Periodic review

- High-risk customers: KYC refresh every 2 years.
- Medium-risk customers: KYC refresh every 8 years.
- Low-risk customers: KYC refresh every 10 years.
