# Delinquency and Collections Playbook (Sample)

> **SYNTHETIC SAMPLE DOCUMENT.** This file is fictional demo data for the Lending Copilot
> portfolio project. Any resemblance to real institutions, products, or persons is coincidental.

## 1. Delinquency buckets

Accounts are bucketed by days past due (DPD):

| Bucket | DPD range | Owner |
|--------|-----------|-------|
| X | 1 - 30 | Tele-calling team |
| 1 | 31 - 60 | Tele-calling + field visit |
| 2 | 61 - 90 | Field collections |
| 3 | 91 - 180 | Senior collections officers |
| Write-off review | 180+ | Credit committee |

## 2. Contact strategy

- First reminder: SMS and email on day 3 of delinquency.
- Tele-calling begins on day 7, with a maximum of 3 attempts per day between 8 AM and 8 PM.
- Field visits start at 31 DPD for ticket sizes above INR 2,00,000.
- Never contact the borrower at their workplace without prior consent.

## 3. Restructuring

Borrowers facing genuine hardship (job loss, medical emergency) may be offered:

- A one-time EMI moratorium of up to 3 months, or
- Tenure extension of up to 12 months with a revised repayment schedule.

Restructuring requires documented proof of hardship and credit manager approval.
A restructured account is flagged as such in bureau reporting.

## 4. Settlement

Settlements are a last resort and require regional credit head approval.
The minimum acceptable settlement is 60% of the outstanding principal for bucket 3 accounts.

## 5. Write-off

Accounts at 180+ DPD with no payment activity for 90 days are recommended for write-off.
Write-off is an accounting treatment only; collection efforts and legal recourse continue.
